I am currently a Staff Engineer at Analog Devices. Previously, I was a Senior Research Engineer at Northeastern University. My work brings together system architecture, RF hardware, embedded systems, signal processing, simulation, integration, and measurement for advanced wireless communication and sensing systems. I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Northeastern University in 2022, my M.S. from Zhejiang University in 2017, and my B.S. from Zhejiang University of Technology in 2014.
